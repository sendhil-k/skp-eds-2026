**Observed Patterns:**
- **Color:** Dark navy (#232F3E) header/footer, white content areas, orange (#FF9900) accent CTAs
- **Typography:** Amazon Ember font family, consistent heading hierarchy
- **Grid:** 4-column card grids, 3-column feature blocks, single-column documentation
- **Navigation:** Global mega-menu (11 categories), contextual breadcrumbs, comprehensive footer (5 sections)
- **Components:** Highly reusable card system, newsletter signup, social links, language selector
- **Spacing:** Consistent vertical rhythm with section dividers
- **Responsive:** Mobile-first with collapsible nav

**Consistency:** High within the portal — all pages share the same header, footer, and structural patterns. Variations are limited to content block composition.
